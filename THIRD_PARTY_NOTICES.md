- **File:** `crates/jetcarp-core/src/hat.rs`
- **Source:** ported from Craig S. Kaplan's [hatviz](https://github.com/isohedral/hatviz)

  monotile" (2023).

- **Files:** `crates/jetcarp-core/src/isohedral/` (tables generated by `tools/gen_isohedral.py`)
- **Source:** converted from Craig S. Kaplan's [tactile-js](https://github.com/isohedral/tactile-js)
- **Licence:** BSD 3-Clause. Copyright (c) 2018, Craig S. Kaplan. The full text is in

### k-uniform tilings

- **Files:** `crates/jetcarp-core/data/tilings/kuniform.json` (generated by
  `tools/gen_kuniform.py`) and `tools/data/JSON_Galebach.json` (the source, unchanged)
- **Source:** J. E. Soto Sánchez's *Galebach collection* (<https://chequesoto.info/tiling/>),
  which encodes Brian Galebach's catalogue of k-uniform tilings
  (<http://www.probabilitysports.com/tilings.html>) in the integer representation of J. E. Soto
  Sánchez, T. Weyrich, A. Medeiros e Sá and L. H. de Figueiredo, "An integer representation for
  periodic tilings of the plane by regular polygons", *Computers & Graphics* 95 (2021) 69–80.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.
- **Changes:** JETCARP converted the data: `tools/gen_kuniform.py` rebuilds each tiling's exact
  geometry, translation lattice, symmetry group and vertex orbits from the source, and writes them
  in JETCARP's own format. The source file is kept unchanged.

### Evolutionary glider search: mutation and rule notation

- **Files:** `crates/jetcarp-core/src/evolve.rs` (`mutate`) and
  `crates/jetcarp-core/src/evolve/priority.rs` (`PriorityRule::notation`)
- **Source:** adapted from James Edmond's [monotile-ca](https://github.com/jamesedmond/monotile-ca)
- **Licence:** MIT (monotile-ca is MIT OR Apache-2.0; JETCARP takes it under MIT). Copyright (c)
  2026 James Edmond. The MIT text is below. The rest of JETCARP's evolutionary search is written
  from Edmond's paper (arXiv:2609.22579) and is JETCARP's own code.

### Lenia pattern *Orbium unicaudatus*

- **File:** `crates/jetcarp-core/src/lenia.rs` (the `ORBIUM` constant only)

## Methods reimplemented without copying code

These carry no licence obligation, but the work they are based on is credited here and in the
documentation:

- **Evolutionary glider search** (`crates/jetcarp-core/src/evolve.rs`, `evolve/`): the method of
  James Edmond, "Gliders on Aperiodic Monotilings: Cellular Automata on the Hat and Spectre"
  (arXiv:2609.22579), written from the paper and a reading of his repository; the two functions
  adapted from that repository are listed above.
- **Spectre glider rule** (`SpectreGlider`): Edmond's published rule (arXiv:2609.22579, Table 7).
- **Von Neumann's 29-state automaton:** written from J. von Neumann, *Theory of Self-Reproducing
  Automata*, ed. A. W. Burks (University of Illinois Press, 1966). No text, figures, rule files or
  patterns from other programs were used.
- **WebP lossless encoder** (`crates/jetcarp-core/src/media/vp8l.rs`): written from the WebP
  Lossless Bitstream Specification (RFC 9649), with no libwebp code.

JETCARP contains no code, rule files or pattern files from Golly.
